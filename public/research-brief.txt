MISSION
Research Khmer OCR that can turn printed text into searchable, usable digital text, while preserving the script's characters, marks, and joiners.

STAGE
OpenKhmer is Neuroshift's Khmer OCR product in development, based in Cambodia. Current work is early-stage research. No public OCR tool, released API, published benchmark results, or sponsorship is claimed.

VERIFIED FOUNDATION
An initial Colab cleaning run for one synthetic printed Khmer dataset was completed. The prepared corpus contains 60,724 training, 3,308 validation, and 3,379 locked test examples. These are data-preparation counts, not model-performance results.

NEXT STEPS
1. Verify the revised preparation workflow and its saved outputs.
2. Run a small-set training gate before broader experiments.
3. Compare recognition approaches on the same data using character error rate.
4. Evaluate fixed configurations on a locked test set.
5. Study ornate lettering and other document styles separately.

SUPPORT SOUGHT
The next milestone is verified Kaggle preparation outputs and a documented 16-example training gate with error analysis. A failed gate will be reported and diagnosed before broader training.
Cloud/GPU credits for controlled training and evaluation.
Technical mentorship in OCR, Khmer language technology, and experimental design.
Startup program support to develop a sustainable path from research to a useful product.

RESEARCH PRINCIPLES
Preserve Unicode and Khmer shaping-related characters. Audit labels and source coverage. Avoid training/test overlap. Keep a locked test set. Measure before scaling. Do not present a diagnostic model run as usable OCR.
