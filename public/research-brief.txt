OPENKHMER — RESEARCH BRIEF
Research snapshot: October 2, 2026

COMPANY
Neuroshift is the Cambodia-based company behind OpenKhmer.
Founded: March 2025
Funding: No funds raised
Project contact: Sophanha Oun
Contact: sophanha@neuroshift.dev
Website source: https://github.com/dvdphobia/openkhmer-web (website code)

MISSION
Research Khmer OCR that can turn printed text into searchable, usable digital
text while preserving the script's characters, marks, and joiners.

STAGE
OpenKhmer is an early-stage research and data-preparation effort and a product
in development. There is no working OCR product, public API, or published
accuracy result.

REPORTED INITIAL PREPARATION
Project notes report a completed initial Colab cleaning run for one synthetic
printed Khmer source. The prepared corpus is reported as 60,724 training,
3,308 validation, and 3,379 locked test examples. These are preparation counts,
not model-performance results. The cloud artifacts are not included here for
independent inspection.

NEXT STEPS
1. Verify the revised preparation workflow and its saved outputs.
2. Run a small-set training gate before broader experiments.
3. Compare recognition approaches on the same data using character error rate.
4. Evaluate fixed configurations on a locked test set.
5. Study ornate lettering and other document styles separately.

PLANNED CLAUDE ASSISTANCE
Claude has been used as a chat assistant during research. Planned use is for
internal research and development assistance. A future document-chat feature
using the Claude API could answer questions grounded in OCR text; it is not
operational. It would need answer evaluation and source checks, and its
usefulness would depend on Khmer OCR accuracy.

SUPPORT SOUGHT
The next milestone is verified preparation outputs and a documented 16-example
training gate with error analysis. A failed gate will be reported and diagnosed
before broader training. Support sought includes cloud/GPU credits, technical
mentorship in OCR and Khmer language technology, and startup program support.
No external sponsorship or program membership is claimed.

RESEARCH PRINCIPLES
Preserve Unicode and Khmer shaping-related characters. Audit labels and source
coverage. Avoid training/test overlap. Keep a locked test set. Measure before
scaling. Do not present a diagnostic model run as usable OCR.
